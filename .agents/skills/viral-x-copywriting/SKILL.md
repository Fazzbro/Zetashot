# Viral X Copywriting Master Skill

A battle-tested, high-density copywriting framework engineered to capture immediate attention in the feed, sustain deep dwell time, maximize bookmark/share conversion, and systematically trigger out-of-network distribution on X.

---

## 1. The Core Law of Copywriting

> **"Good copywriting is getting the right person to take the right action."**

Every post on X is an engineered conversion funnel. That action might be reading the next line, expanding a thread, bookmarking for reference, leaving a heated reply, reposting to peers, or buying a product. If a post does not direct a specific human to take a specific action, it is merely noise.

---

## 2. Foundational Principles

Before writing a single word, verify the four non-negotiable fundamentals:

### I. Write for a Specific Avatar at a Specific Funnel Stage
Picture your ideal reader with photographic clarity:
- *What specific bottleneck are they struggling with right now?*
- *What vocabulary and jargon do they use when venting to peers?*
- *What emotional state are they in (skeptical, ambitious, exhausted, curious)?*

```
               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │ TOP OF FUNNEL (TOF) — 60%–70% of Output                │
               │ Target: Cold/Broad Audience | Goal: Mass Reach         │
               │ Copy Style: Short, punchy, universal friction/utility  │
               └──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                          │
               ┌──────────────────────────▼─────────────────────────────┐
               │ MIDDLE OF FUNNEL (MOF) — 20%–30% of Output             │
               │ Target: Warm Peers/Followers | Goal: Deep Trust        │
               │ Copy Style: Audited proof, frameworks, case studies    │
               └──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                          │
               ┌──────────────────────────▼─────────────────────────────┐
               │ BOTTOM OF FUNNEL (BOF) — 10% of Output                 │
               │ Target: Hot Buyers/Clients | Goal: Conversion/Revenue  │
               │ Copy Style: Maximum technical specificity, direct CTA  │
               └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
> **The Golden Funnel Law**: *The closer you get to a sale, the more detail you need.*

### II. One Single Idea Per Post
- **One thesis, one post**. If you have three distinct insights, you have three distinct posts.
- Mixing multiple unrelated thoughts confuses the reader and destroys the semantic vector mapping in X's recommendation engine (**Simcluster**).

### III. Every Post Must Deliver a Concrete Takeaway
Ask before hitting publish: *"What does the reader walk away with?"*
- A new mental model, an immediate tactical checklist, or an unburdened belief.
- Never write open-ended philosophical musings that leave the reader thinking *"So what?"*.

### IV. Clarity > Cleverness (The 12-Year-Old Friction Rule)
- Good writing reduces cognitive friction.
- If a 12-year-old cannot comprehend your sentence structure, vocabulary, and flow, do not publish it.
- This does **not** mean dumbing down technical concepts; it means stripping out pretentious academic vocabulary, convoluted subordinate clauses, and corporate jargon.

## 3. The Anatomy of a Master Hook (Hooks 101)

The hook is the highest-leverage asset in written media.

> **The Single Law of Hooks**: *The only job of Line 1 is to force Line 2 to be read. The only job of Line 2 is to force Line 3 to be read.*

### The 4-Part Master Hook Anatomy (Dissected)

```
[PART 1: DESIRED OUTCOME]   ──▶ "Nail this, and you'll never struggle with X growth ever again."
[PART 2: UNCOMFORTABLE PAIN] ──▶ "The brutal reality is, most brands on X are doomed from the start."
[PART 3: CORE ROOT SOLUTION] ──▶ "...because they're missing one key skill: The art of turning brilliant ideas into words people want to read."
[PART 4: PROOF & AUTHORITY]  ──▶ "For the past 6 years, I've obsessed over this. It took me to 1B+ impressions and +9,000 followers in Month 1."
```

### The 8 Master Hook Typologies
1. **Bold Claim**: *"Copywriting is the single most asymmetric skill in technology."*
2. **Identity Callout**: *"If you're an indie founder with under 1K followers, read this."*
3. **Curiosity Gap**: *"I just noticed a subtle shift that 99% of creators are completely missing."*
4. **Clear Desired Outcome**: *"How to write posts that hit the top 1% of the 'For You' feed."*
5. **Contrarian Pattern Interrupt**: *"Posting volume is a trap. You need to post 70% less."*
6. **Origin / Vulnerability Story**: *"It took me 6 years and $40,000 in mistakes to realize this..."*
7. **Numbered Listicle**: *"7 copywriting rules I wish I had when starting from 0."*
8. **Audited Proof**: *"This single video generated 2.4M impressions. Here is the full teardown:"*

### The 5 Psychological Levers & Multi-Lever Stacking
Every top-performing hook pulls at least one of these 5 human levers. The highest-performing hooks **stack 2 or 3 simultaneously**:

| Lever | Human Driver | High-Performance Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Self-Interest** | "What's in it for me?" (Money, time, status) | *"This one pricing change doubled my software margin."* |
| **Curiosity** | An open loop the brain refuses to ignore | *"I almost quit my business at 500 users. Then I discovered this."* |
| **Novelty** | Something never heard before | *"The 2026 X algorithm just deprecated traditional thread timing."* |
| **Emotion / Tension** | Fear of loss, indignation, urgency | *"You are one platform algorithm update away from zero revenue."* |
| **Identity** | "This is for people like me" | *"If you're a bootstrapped founder building in public, stop scrolling."* |

> **Stacking Formula**: `[Identity Callout]` + `[Self-Interest Outcome]` + `[Curiosity Gap]`  
> *"If you're a founder with under 1K followers (Identity), this one formatting shift doubled my impressions in 48 hours (Self-Interest + Curiosity)."*

## 4. Format-Specific Hook Rules

- **Short Posts (< 280 characters)**: The hook **is** the post. Line 1 must stand completely alone as a quotable anchor.
- **Threads**: Hook + explicit promise of the curriculum (`"Here are the 7 non-negotiable rules:"`).
- **Long-Form Articles**: Title + Subtitle + Opening Paragraph. Treat all three as independent nested hooks.
- **Video Content**: The first **1.5 seconds** determine 90% of total views. Require **visual motion + bold on-screen typography + immediate spoken hook**. Zero logo intro animations or throat-clearing intros.
- **Quote Tweets**: Never just say *"This"* or *"Big if true"*. You must add novel tension, counter-intuitive context, or distill the core thesis.
- **Graphics / Mockups**: The headline inside the visual asset is the hook. Must be readable at thumbnail size on mobile screens.

## 6. The 6 Core Post Structures

Formatting and structure are visual extensions of the hook. If a post looks daunting, the user scrolls.

### 1. Problem 𝕏 Insight 𝕏 Takeaway (PIT)
*Structure*: Name a common failure → expose what people misunderstand → give a tactical directive.
```markdown
Bad advice that is popular for some reason:

"Stay extremely niche and avoid broad reach."

First principles: attract widespread attention so mass opportunities emerge.

Staying niche to hit your first $10k/mo is fine. But broad reach builds the long-term enterprise moat.
```

### 2. The High-Density Listicle
*Structure*: Bold 1-line hook → 5–10 short imperative bullet lines → authoritative closer.
- Easy to scan, easy to save, drives massive bookmarks.
- Use clean visual anchors (`•`, `<`, `→`).

### 3. The Graphic Packaging Post
*Structure*: 1-line minimalist hook + high-aesthetic visual asset.
- The image or mockup does 80% of the heavy lifting.
- Forces dwell time as the viewer zooms into the details.

### 4. The Timecoded Video Masterclass
*Structure*: Direct outcome hook + full chapter outline with timestamps + embedded video.
- Maximizes **Dwell Time** (the highest multiplier in X's Phoenix ranking engine).

### 5. The Long-Form In-Depth Article
*Structure*: Authoritative headline + modular subheaders + high-density paragraphs + actionable summary.
- Builds undeniable authority and converts cold visitors into loyal subscribers.

### 6. The Quote Tweet Value Layer
*Structure*: High-conviction philosophical declaration + aspirational framing + explicit command to bookmark the quoted resource.

## 7. Rhythm & Mobile Readability Protocol

Readers do not read posts; **they scan them** on vertical mobile displays.

### The Gary Provost Rhythm Rule
Vary sentence lengths deliberately. Monotonous sentences put readers to sleep:
```markdown
This sentence has five words. Here are five more words. Five-word sentences are okay. 
No! Listen.

Now write a short sentence. Then write another short sentence of moderate length. 
And sometimes, when you want the reader's heart to race and their brain to lock into your momentum, 
write a sentence that flows with unmistakable power and rhythm. 

Then stop. Hit them hard.
```

### The Scanability Checklist
- **1 to 3 Lines Per Paragraph**: On mobile, a 4-line paragraph looks like an impenetrable wall of text.
- **One Thought Per Line**: If a sentence has two "and"s and a comma, split it into two sentences.
- **Visual Sinking Bridges**: Short connector lines that pull the eyes down the page:
  - *"Let me explain..."*
  - *"Here's the brutal reality:"*
  - *"Trust me on this:"*
  - *"Notice the pattern?"*
- **Whitespace is Oxygen**: Generous line breaks prevent cognitive fatigue.

## 8. Word Choice & Ruthless Deletion (Anti-AI-Slop)

Every word must fight for its right to stay on the page.

### I. The Ruthless Deletion Rule
> If deleting a word does not alter the core meaning of the sentence, **delete it immediately**.

### II. Purge Low-Conviction Filler
- Cut: `really`, `very`, `just`, `actually`, `in order to`, `basically`, `somewhat`.

### III. Simple Words Over Academic Inflation
- `utilise` → **use**
- `facilitate` → **help**
- `commence` → **start**
- `demonstrate` → **show**
- `leverage` (when used as a lazy verb) → **use / apply**

### IV. Strong Kinetic Verbs Over Passive Nominalizations
- *"The company experienced very rapid growth"* → *"The company **exploded**."*
- *"Mistakes were made by the team"* → *"The team **blew it**."*

### V. Eliminate AI Slop Patterns
- Strip em-dashes (`—`) used as lazy transitions.
- Ban cliché synthetic templates: *"It's not about X, it's about Y"*, *"In today's fast-paced digital world"*, *"A testament to"*, *"Delve into the tapestry of"*.

### VI. Maintain "You" Dominance (Second-Person Orientation)
- Ratio: **80% "You" / 20% "I"**.
- Always frame insights around the reader's wallet, status, time, and problems.

## 9. Voice Reverse-Engineering & The Content Remix Engine

Refer to [rhetoric_and_remix_guide.md](file:///d:/mockup/.agents/skills/viral-x-copywriting/references/rhetoric_and_remix_guide.md) for full rhetorical teardowns.

### The Remixing Matrix
```
MODE 1: SAME STRUCTURE, DIFFERENT TOPIC
"Rich people buy time. Poor people buy stuff."
  ↳ (Health): "Athletes chase recovery. Amateurs chase fatigue."
  ↳ (SaaS): "Monopolies buy distribution. Startups build features."

MODE 2: SAME TOPIC, DIFFERENT STRUCTURE
"Rich people buy time. Poor people buy stuff."
  ↳ (If/Then): "If you want to build wealth, buy time. If you want to stay poor, buy stuff."
  ↳ (Antithesis): "The poor trade their hours for toys. The rich trade their cash for freedom."
```

### The Two-Banger Fusion
Combine two semi-outlier concepts into a single viral banger:
- Concept A: *"Who you marry will have a bigger impact on your life than what you do for a living."*
- Concept B: *"I'm 32. Trust me on this:"*
- **Fused Mega-Hit**:
  > *"I'm 32. Trust me on this: Nothing will impact your life more than who you marry."*

### Aspirational Identity Framing
Describe an action through the lens of a high-status tribe:
- *Weak*: *"Make decisions quickly and execute."*
- *Aspirational*: *"How to spot a top 1% founder: Watch how fast they make decisions, eliminate blockers, and turn raw ideas into shipped code."*

## 10. CTAs & Funnel Closers

The closing line is the **second most critical sentence** in your post. Never let a post trail off aimlessly.

### Direct the Action Explicitly:
- **For Replies**: *"Which of these 3 do you disagree with?"*
- **For Saves/Bookmarks**: *"Bookmark this so you don't lose the framework when building."*
- **For Shares**: *"Send this to a founder who's struggling with distribution."*
- **For Follows**: *"Follow @[handle] for weekly first-principles breakdowns on product and growth."*
- **For Conversions / DMs**: *"DM me 'system' and I'll send you the Notion template directly."*

### The Reply-Pinning Tactic
- Never put external links in the original tweet (heavily penalized by X's recommendation engine).
- Post your main tweet as clean media/text.
- Immediately comment the CTA link in the **first reply** to capture intent without algorithmic suppression.

## 11. Pre-Flight 10-Gate Viral Copywriting Checklist

Before publishing any tweet, thread, or video hook, run this strict 10-gate audit:

- [ ] **Gate 1: The Hook Tension Test**: Does Line 1 create an irresistible curiosity gap or pattern interrupt?
- [ ] **Gate 2: Audience Clarity**: Is it immediately clear within the first 5 words who this is for?
- [ ] **Gate 3: Single Thesis**: Does the post focus exclusively on ONE idea?
- [ ] **Gate 4: Specifics Over Vague Promises**: Are there concrete numbers, timelines, or proof points?
- [ ] **Gate 5: The 12-Year-Old Friction Test**: Is the vocabulary and sentence structure effortless to scan?
- [ ] **Gate 6: Mobile Line-Break Audit**: Are all paragraphs strictly between 1 and 3 lines?
- [ ] **Gate 7: Gary Provost Rhythm Check**: Are sentence lengths varied dynamically?
- [ ] **Gate 8: Anti-AI-Slop Cleanse**: Are em-dashes, filler adverbs, and synthetic clichés eradicated?
- [ ] **Gate 9: Algorithmic Trigger**: Does the post intentionally trigger Bookmarks (high utility) or Replies (debate)?
- [ ] **Gate 10: Clear Closer & CTA**: Does the last line explicitly direct the reader on what to do next?
